What changed (3 item(s))

  1. Governing statute re-verified: Texas Cottage Food Law (Health & Safety Code ch. 437)
    No change to the law itself this month — but the statute is the floor everything else stands on, so we re-read it every cycle. Chapter 437 of the Health & Safety Code is what allows you to sell specific homemade foods from home. It sets the allowed-food list, the annual gross-income cap, and the exact statements your labels must carry.
    Citation: Tex. Health & Safety Code ch. 437
    Source: https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/HS/htm/HS.437.htm
    Action: Confirm the label on every product you sell still carries the statements Chapter 437 requires, including your home address and the 'not inspected' statement.
    Deadline: 2026-10-01
    File at: https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/HS/htm/HS.437.htm
  2. DSHS cottage food rule re-verified: 25 TAC § 229.661
    No rule change this month. The DSHS rule (25 TAC § 229.661) is the agency's operating detail under the statute — food-handler training, labeling specifics, and how the sales cap is administered. We re-check it every cycle so you do not have to.
    Citation: 25 Tex. Admin. Code § 229.661
    Source: https://www.dshs.texas.gov/food-manufacturers-wholesalers/cottage-food-industry
    Action: Review the DSHS cottage food page and confirm your food-handler training certificate is current.
    Deadline: 2026-10-15
    File at: https://www.dshs.texas.gov/food-manufacturers-wholesalers/cottage-food-industry
